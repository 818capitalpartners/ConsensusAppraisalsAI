Real Estate Operator Bio

Guided Questionnaire for Commercial Loan Applications

Instructions This questionnaire helps lenders understand your real estate experience, track record, and operational capability. Your responses will be compiled into a professional operator biography for your loan package.

Be specific: include numbers, dollar amounts, dates, and property addresses where possible. Lenders want evidence, not generalities.
1 Personal & Entity Information

List every LLC, LP, Corp, or trust you use for real estate. Include state of formation and your role (managing member, GP, etc.).

Phone, email, website if applicable.

2 Real Estate Experience & Track Record

Examples: ground-up construction, gut renovations, cosmetic flips, buy-and-hold rentals, commercial repositioning, multifamily value-add, mixed-use development, land entitlement.

Include purchases, renovations, dispositions. Break down by type if possible.

Combined acquisition + renovation costs across your career.

For EACH project include: property address, acquisition date, purchase price, renovation budget (planned vs. actual), scope of work, timeline, exit strategy and sale price OR current value/rent if held, and key challenges you overcame.

3 Current Portfolio & Active Projects

Include properties under contract. Break down: residential, multifamily, commercial, etc.

For each: address, scope, budget, expected completion date, exit plan.

Approximate is fine. Should align with your Net Worth Statement and REO Schedule.

4 Team & Operational Capability

If self-managed: describe your process. If third-party: name the PM company and how many units they manage for you.

List key people: GC, attorney, CPA, property manager, architect, insurance broker, partners. Include how long you have worked with each.

Licensed GC, real estate broker, architect, engineer, etc.

Direct marketing, broker relationships, off-market, auctions, MLS, networking, etc.

5 Financial Profile & Lending History

Hard money, private lending, DSCR, conventional, portfolio, seller financing, syndication, etc. Name lenders if possible.

If yes, explain circumstances and outcome. If no, state None. Lenders will verify this.

e.g., 680-720. If below 680, briefly explain context.

Cash reserves, lines of credit, or other liquid assets available for down payments, carry costs, and contingencies.

6 This Specific Transaction

Property address, type, current condition, acquisition price or refinance amount.

Renovation scope, timeline, budget, target ARV or stabilized NOI, exit strategy (sell, refi, hold).

Down payment amount, source of funds, any co-investors or partners.

Connect your experience to THIS deal. What in your track record proves you can execute this specific business plan?

7 Additional Information

Relevant professional background, awards, community involvement, or anything that strengthens your profile.

List 2-3 professional references: name, relationship, phone/email. Ideally: a past lender, a GC, and a broker or attorney.

Certification

I certify that the information provided in this questionnaire is true, complete, and accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand this information will be used to prepare an operator biography as part of a commercial loan application.
